How to read this page. These are statistics about public SEC filings, not a recommendation and not a portfolio to copy. The index is hypothetical: it shows how the stocks held in common moved, equal weighted, between quarter ends, using the prices implied by the 13Fs (value ÷ shares), corrected for stock splits, without dividends or costs. 13Fs only show US-listed long positions at quarter end and are filed up to 45 days later, so nobody could have known the members on the day the quarter started. Stocks that stop appearing in any 13F leave the calculation. Past movements say nothing about future ones. Information, not investment advice.

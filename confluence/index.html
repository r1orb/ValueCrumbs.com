SUPERINVESTORS + INSIDERS

Confluence

Stocks that superinvestors and the company's own insiders are both buying, each group in its own SEC filing. Two independent sets of buyers putting money into the same company is rarer than either one alone. The more buyers on each side, the higher the level.

Level 3 Double cluster A group on both sides: 2+ superinvestors and 3+ insiders.
Level 2 Strong match A group on one side (2+ superinvestors or 3+ insiders) and at least one buyer on the other.
Level 1 Match At least one superinvestor and one insider.
